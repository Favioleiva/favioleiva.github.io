Hello! I'm Favio, an economist and PhD student in International Development at Nagoya University. My research explores economic development, mining, agriculture, ethnicity, and inequality in Peru using spatial econometrics, remote sensing, and large-scale data. I also develop open-source tools for GPU-accelerated statistical and geospatial analysis, with broader interests in economic history, Asia-Pacific development, and scientific computing.
